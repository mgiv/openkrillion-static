# OpenKrillion models and game capabilities

Canonical: https://openkrillion.com/models.md

No generative AI model is available or called by this game. The models.md filename follows the agent documentation convention; this is a trivia game, not an AI generation workspace.

## Available workflows
- Daily seven-prompt trivia
- Practice dives
- Six rarity tiers
- Typo correction with confirmation
- Animated ocean descent
- Synthesized sound effects
- Score and depth report
- Answer review
- Local history and streaks
- Shareable results
- Low-power and colorblind modes

Gameplay language: English. Help and AI questions: English and Chinese.

Answers are validated locally in the browser against the bundled sample entries, with normalization, known aliases and conservative typo correction. This static edition uses a small sample bank for both daily and practice play; it does not fetch official daily puzzles or synchronize reference data.

No accounts, multiplayer, themed packs, checkout or cross-device account sync is implemented. Practice does not change the daily score or streak.
