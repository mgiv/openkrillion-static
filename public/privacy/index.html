Privacy at OpenKrillion

This independent game runs in your browser without an account. Game progress is stored on this device only. This page describes the static edition.

Game progress

Preferences, draft answers, your in-progress dive, feedback and completed history are stored locally in your browser. No game answers, scores or identifiers are sent to a game server. Clearing browser storage removes the local copy. There is no cross-device login or account sync.

Statistics and feedback

Daily score comparisons and feedback submission are not available in this static edition. The results screen shows your score without comparing it to other players.

Analytics

This static edition does not load analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Your answers and game activity remain in this browser.

Hosting and references

The game and its sample puzzle bank are delivered as static files. It does not connect to a database, reference service, or game API.

Cookies

The game does not set cookies. Browser local storage is used for settings, drafts and history.

Retention and requests

Local game records remain in this browser until you clear them or use your browser's site-data controls. No payment information is collected.

External AI links

AI icons open third-party services with a public product question. Opening them shares that question with the selected provider. No game answer, identifier or private history is included.
